Portraits of the Basilisk

[paintings & cryptoart]

Portraits of the Basilisk explores the cultural fantasies projected onto artificial intelligence. Developed from images produced by an obsolete generative model and painted by hand in Procreate, the portraits bring together bodies, technological remnants and the promise of machine transcendence.
