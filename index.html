Marcus Couto, also known as Marcvs, is a Brazilian multidisciplinary artist based in São Paulo. He works with image, text and sound, using painting, software and artificial intelligence models to build provocative narratives about the present: the ontological impacts of the unbridled advance of technology and hypercapitalism. He describes himself as a “cartographer of the technocracy.” He is the creator of Zombiebasilisk, a project of AI-made horror and found-footage shorts that has drawn more than 18 million views. He was a finalist for the Ibero-American Visual Arts Prize (CERLALC/UNESCO) for his work with AI-generated images, with an exhibition in Bogotá. In experimental electronic music, his project Osasco Dynamics was reviewed by The Wire magazine.

Films

/zombiebasilisk

The audiovisual work of Marcvs takes shape under the name Zombiebasilisk: a channel of AI-made horror and found footage, on TikTok and Instagram since 2025. The films arrive as found documents: images the brain can’t immediately catalog, anchored in something familiar. Found footage instead of “cinematic”. 480p instead of upscaling. The archival aesthetic is a decision, not a limitation.
